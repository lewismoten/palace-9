# Noughtlear Proliferation — use boundary

This is a small educational tic-tac-toe simulation about optimal play, tied outcomes, and the limits of winning against an equally capable opponent.

It is not a weapons system, targeting system, command-and-control component, or a validation tool for military operations. It must not be used to train, operate, optimize, or evaluate weapons of mass destruction or other real-world weapon systems.

The intended use is learning: inspecting optimal-move distributions, reproducible randomized tie resolution, routing in a tiny mixture-of-experts demonstration, and the distinction between a forced draw in a toy game and real-world strategic decisions.
